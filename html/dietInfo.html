Flying Squirrel Diet

Dietary Habits

Flying squirrels are nocturnal omnivores. Southern flying squirrels are considered one of the most carnivorous squirrel species because they frequently supplement their diet with meat and eggs.

What They Eat

  • Seeds & Nuts: Core part of their daily foraging.
  • Fungi & Fruit: Gathered from tree canopies and forest floors.
  • Insects & Animals: Insects, bird eggs, live birds, and carrion (animal remains).

How Much and How Often

  • Frequency: Forage at night (nocturnal) to avoid daytime predators like hawks.
  • Amount: Eats a varied seasonal diet; multiple squirrels often nest together in winter to keep warm when food is scarce.
