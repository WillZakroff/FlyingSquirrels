About Flying Squirrels Behavior

Where on earth do Flying Squirrels live?

Flying squirrels live in forests and woodlands across North America, Central America, northern Europe, and Asia

What natural surroundings does the animal need to thrive?

Flying squirrels need mature, dense forests with a continuous tree canopy and plenty of old, hollow trees to thrive. Because they do not actually fly but glide, their survival depends heavily on specific woodland structures that allow them to move safely and find food.

Where does the animal make their home?

Flying squirrels nest in natural tree cavities, old woodpecker holes, snags, and human-made nest boxes.
